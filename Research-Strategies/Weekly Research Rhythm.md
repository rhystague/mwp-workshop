# Weekly Research Rhythm

Use this as a lightweight weekly review. Keep it short enough to complete in 15-30 minutes.

## Checklist

- [ ] Scan new alerts or Zotero additions.
- [ ] Triage 1-3 papers: Keep, Skim later, Cite only, or Discard.
- [ ] Process one useful paper into `Paper-Notes/`.
- [ ] Add 1-3 distilled insights to a map note in `Maps/`.
- [ ] Advance one `Potential-Papers/` note with a next action.
- [ ] Write one short weekly research insight.

## Weekly research insight

```markdown
## Weekly research insight - {{date}}

This week I noticed that ...

This matters because ...

The next question is ...
```
